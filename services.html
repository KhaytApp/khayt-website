Online services

Khayt is a desktop app that works with no account, no server and no internet. On top of that there is an optional, end-to-end-encrypted cloud. This page is what it is, what each part of it sends, and how to switch any of it off.

Plans

The prices are real. Nobody is being charged yet.

The desktop app is free forever and always will be — it is the hosted service, and only the hosted service, that Khayt charges for. Those prices are published now so nobody discovers one later, having built their shop on the assumption there wasn't one.

SAR is pegged to USD at 3.75, so these are the same price expressed twice. Other locales see USD — a rate snapshot that goes stale is worse than a foreign currency.

Off is the default, not a fallback

Every service below ships switched off. A shop that never turns one on gets the whole app — queue, quoting, invoicing, inventory, analytics — running on its own machine, with no account to create and nothing leaving the building. Turning a service off again returns the app to exactly that.

The sync model

The server holds ciphertext it cannot read

Sync is end-to-end encrypted. Your shop's data is encrypted on your own machine, with a sync passphrase that never leaves it, and the server stores the result. That is the whole arrangement: the server's copy is what lets a second machine catch up, and it is not a copy anyone at the other end can open.

The consequence worth knowing before you switch it on is the one that follows from the design: a lost passphrase cannot be reset for you. There is no recovery path that does not involve you, because a recovery path that did not would mean the server could read the data.

Where this stands

All of it is beta, and labelled that way in the app

The cloud is marked BETA on the home page, in the app and here, and the word is meant literally rather than as a disclaimer: these services are newer than the offline core, they change between releases, and a shop that needs its book to be boring should run the offline core and leave them alone.

Nothing is being charged today: every plan is free while the cloud is in beta, and billing will not switch on before payment collection exists. The mode comparison on the home page is a different thing entirely — it describes what each mode shows you locally, not what you have paid for.

If you are weighing one of these up, the honest order is: run Khayt offline first, decide whether the thing you want is worth an account at all, and switch on only that one.

ولا يُحصَّل شيء اليوم: كل خطة مجانية ما دامت السحابة تجريبية، ولن تبدأ الفوترة قبل أن توجد وسيلة تحصيل أصلاً. أمّا مقارنة الأوضاع في الصفحة الرئيسية فشيء آخر تماماً — فهي تصف ما يعرضه كل وضع على جهازك، لا ما دفعت مقابله.

وإن كنت توازن بين هذه الخدمات، فالترتيب الصادق هو: شغّل خيط دون اتصال أولاً، وقرّر إن كان ما تريده يستحق حساباً أصلاً، ثم شغّل ذلك وحده.
