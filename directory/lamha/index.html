Lamha shipping

by Khayt Reviewed and signed

Records a job as an order in your Lamha account, ready for you to book a courier there. It never books or pays for a shipment by itself.

Install in Khayt: coming

What it sends, and where

When it runs, this connector sends these fields, and only these, to these addresses:

  • app.lamha.saother content, address, name, phone number, email address, purchase history

It may also: fill in its own fields

Its keys stay on this computer. Nothing is sent until you press one of its buttons or an event it listens to happens.

These rows are worked out from the signed manifest when this page is built, the same way the app builds its consent sheet. Khayt shows you the same list before anything is installed.
