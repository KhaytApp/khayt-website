Billing will not begin without notice. The plans, and what each service transmits, are set out on the online services page.

Plans during the beta. Every shop starts on the Free plan. The shop’s owner can move the shop to another plan in the app, which shows each plan’s price, VAT included. To keep the plan after that, you will need to start paying for it; if you don’t, the shop moves back to Free. Moving back deletes nothing. Products can no longer be made, changed or released, and they are no longer listed on makerrun.com. A sale of a product already released still delivers, and buyers who have paid keep their links. Once the beta has ended, moving up for free is no longer possible.
