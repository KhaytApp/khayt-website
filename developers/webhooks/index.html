Shop webhooks

A shop can send its own events to any https address it sets in Settings — a Zapier or Make hook, or your own server. This is what arrives.

The body

The request body is an outer object — event, payload and timestamp in milliseconds — with the delivery inside payload. The delivery’s id stays the same on every retry and on a resend from the delivery log, so you can ignore one you have already handled.

Checking a signature

When the shop has set a signing secret, three headers are added: X-Khayt-Signature (HMAC-SHA256 of the body, lowercase hex, no prefix), X-Khayt-Timestamp (Unix seconds) and X-Khayt-Signature-V2 (HMAC-SHA256 of <timestamp>.<body>). With no secret, none of them is sent. Check V2 with the same recipe connectors use.

Delivery

Up to 5 attempts — at once, then after 30 seconds, 2 minutes, 10 minutes and 1 hour — until a 2xx. 410 Gone stops the retries and switches the subscription off. Retries wait on the shop’s computer and survive a restart, so they are only sent while it is on.
