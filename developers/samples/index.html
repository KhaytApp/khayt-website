Sample connectors

Connectors Khayt has written, reviewed and signed. Copy one as the start of your own.

Draft. Connectors are not released yet. Each sample below is checked when this page is built: it passes the app’s validator and its signature verifies against a published key. The requests are built by the app’s own code. Secrets in a URL are shown as YOUR-….

Discord alerts

Posts to a Discord channel when a job comes in or ships. It reacts to the order_created event and the order_shipped event. File: plugins/samples/discord-notifier.json, signed with khayt-2026-11.

What Discord alerts sends
HostFields
discord.comorder.carrier Purchases › Purchase History
order.number Purchases › Purchase History
order.title User Content › Other User Content
order.trackingNumber Purchases › Purchase History

The request for the order_created event, for a sample order:

POST https://discord.com/api/webhooks/YOUR-WEBHOOKPATH
Content-Type: application/json

{"content":"New job INV-2026-0107: Bracket set"}

Lamha shipping

Records a job as an order in your Lamha account, ready for you to book a courier there. It never books or pays for a shipment by itself. It reacts to a record button. File: plugins/samples/lamha-shipping.json, signed with khayt-2026-11.

What Lamha shipping sends
HostFields
app.lamha.saconfig.carrierId User Content › Other User Content
config.senderAddress Contact Info › Physical Address
config.senderCity User Content › Other User Content
config.senderName Contact Info › Name
config.senderPhone Contact Info › Phone Number
customer.address Contact Info › Physical Address
customer.email Contact Info › Email Address
customer.name Contact Info › Name
customer.phone Contact Info › Phone Number
field.city User Content › Other User Content
order.currency Purchases › Purchase History
order.date Purchases › Purchase History
order.id Purchases › Purchase History
order.number Purchases › Purchase History
order.qty Purchases › Purchase History
order.title User Content › Other User Content
order.total Purchases › Purchase History

The request for a record button, for a sample order:

POST https://app.lamha.sa/api/v2/create-order
Content-Type: application/json

{"ShipmentCurrency":"SAR","carrier_id":"Riyadh","create_shippment":false,"customer":{"AddressLine1":"King Fahd Rd, Riyadh","City":null,"Country":"SA","email":null,"name":"Sara A.","phone":"+966500000000","phone1":"+966500000000"},"date":null,"items":[{"amount":180,"name":"Bracket set","quantity":null,"weight":"1"}],"order_id":"INV-2026-0107","parcels":"1","payment_method":"prepaid","reference_id":"ORD-1042","shipper":{"AddressLine1":"Olaya St, Riyadh","City":"Riyadh","Country":"SA","name":"Athar Studio","phone":"+966511111111"},"total":180}

Google Sheets order log

Adds a row to a Google Sheet for every new and paid job, through an Apps Script web app you deploy. It reacts to the order_created event and the payment_received event. File: plugins/samples/sheets-log.json, signed with khayt-2026-11.

What Google Sheets order log sends
HostFields
script.google.comcustomer.name Contact Info › Name
order.currency Purchases › Purchase History
order.date Purchases › Purchase History
order.number Purchases › Purchase History
order.title User Content › Other User Content
order.total Purchases › Purchase History

The request for the order_created event, for a sample order:

POST https://script.google.com/macros/s/YOUR-DEPLOYMENTID/exec
Content-Type: application/json

{"data":{"customer.name":"Sara A.","order.currency":"SAR","order.date":null,"order.number":"INV-2026-0107","order.title":"Bracket set","order.total":180},"khayt":"plugin-payload-v1","plugin":"app.khayt.samples.sheets","trigger":{"event":"order_created"},"version":"1.0.0"}

Slack alerts

Posts a message to a Slack channel when a job comes in or is paid. It reacts to the order_created event and the payment_received event. File: plugins/samples/slack-notifier.json, signed with khayt-2026-11.

What Slack alerts sends
HostFields

order.currency Purchases › Purchase History
order.number Purchases › Purchase History
order.title User Content › Other User Content
order.total Purchases › Purchase History

The request for the order_created event, for a sample order:

{"text":"New job INV-2026-0107: Bracket set for Sara A. (180 SAR)"}
